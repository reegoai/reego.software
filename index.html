What we deliver

Web engineering

Production-grade web applications: APIs, admin tools, and customer-facing products. Common stacks include Node.js, Python (Django, FastAPI), and C#/.NET on the server; React and modern TypeScript on the client. Focus on security, performance, and maintainability—not buzzwords.

Applications & integrations

Web and desktop applications, internal tools, Slack and messaging integrations, and public APIs. We work the way your team already works: your repos, your environments, and documentation that supports handover.

AI & LLM integration

Practical use of large language models in real products: automation, copilots, and premium features for customers.

  • Tooling and workflows for internal teams
  • Prompt and context design tuned to your domain
  • Function calling, embeddings, and retrieval (vector stores) where they add measurable value

Cloud & data platforms

AWS and Google Cloud: services your product already depends on, wired correctly the first time. Experience with data pipelines, warehousing (e.g. BigQuery, Redshift), containers, and serverless where appropriate.

Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers on how we work, deliver, and bill—no fine print gymnastics.

We ship production systems across common B2B stacks—for example C#/.NET, Node.js, and Python; React, Angular, and TypeScript; SQL and document databases; Docker and Kubernetes; messaging (e.g. Kafka, RabbitMQ). On cloud we work primarily on AWS and Google Cloud, including data platforms such as BigQuery and Redshift where your product needs them.

Most work is defined in a statement of work: clear outcomes, milestones, or a bounded phase of delivery. Structure follows the problem—whether that is milestone-based delivery, a short discovery followed by build, or time and materials with a capped budget. We align on cadence, access, and acceptance criteria before writing code.

Most often we work in your accounts and tools—Git hosting, CI, ticketing, and chat—so access, security, and integrations match how your team already operates. If a greenfield prototype is faster in an isolated space first, we can agree that up front and then transfer cleanly into your org.

Exact assignment is spelled out in the contract. In typical B2B engagements you receive the codebase and rights you need to run and extend the product; we do not hold back a separate “vendor copy” or claim ongoing ownership of your product IP.
